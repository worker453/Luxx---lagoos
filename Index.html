Three reasons people stay till 1am

No single thing makes a night out — it's the seat, the drink, and the sound together.

Outdoor seating

Tables under the open sky

No walls, no rush. Strings of brass-toned light hang over the courtyard, and the Lagos night does the rest — warm air, low music, room to talk.

Cocktails

Poured properly, every time

Classic builds alongside house originals, made to order at the bar and carried out to your table generously measured.

Live music

A band, not a playlist

Live sets turn dinner into a set list. Expect Afrobeats, highlife and a horn section that doesn't hold back.
